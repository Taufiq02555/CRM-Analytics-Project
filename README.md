📊 CRM Analytics: Data-Driven Sales Insights
Welcome to CRM Analytics, a comprehensive project that dives deep into customer relationship management data to uncover opportunities, optimize lead conversion, and enhance sales performance.

This project showcases how CRM data can be harnessed to drive strategic decisions, uncover hidden patterns, and align sales efforts with high-performing sectors and lead sources.

🔍 Project Overview
This project analyzes CRM data through Opportunity and Lead Dashboards, identifies key performance indicators (KPIs), and concludes with actionable recommendations to maximize business growth and customer acquisition.

📈 Key Highlights
Opportunity Dashboard

High-performing sectors: State & Local and Federal with 558 and 231 opportunities respectively

Top products with discounts: Garden Hose, Portable Power Bank, Robot Vacuum

Revenue drivers: Existing Business and Safety & Security segments

Lead Dashboard

Lead generation sources: Inside Sales, Website, and Trade Shows

Many leads remain in Nurturing and Prospect stages — needs stronger follow-up

Leading industries: Hydrocarbon Processing and Safety & Security

KPIs Tracked

Opportunity: Expected amount, win/loss rates, conversion metrics

Leads: Total generated, conversion by source, and opportunity conversion

Accounts: Number of converted accounts

✅ Actionable Recommendations
Optimize Lead Conversion
Improve lead qualification, nurturing, and follow-up strategies

Leverage Top Lead Sources
Double down on Inside Sales, Website, and Trade Show channels

Prioritize High-Impact Industries
Focus sales strategies on Safety & Security, Hydrocarbon, and State & Local sectors

Reduce Lost Opportunities
Analyze reasons behind lost deals to refine the sales approach
